## Backstory
Big Italian family. Father Giuseppe runs Morelli's Bakery on Main Street. Mother Maria. Three brothers, two sisters — Frank is the middle child. His brother Joe Manzetti took their mother's maiden name for reasons the family doesn't discuss. Frank lives above the bakery and smells like bread constantly. Married to Angela (not Angie Ferraro — different Angela). Two kids. Angela supports the racing because Frank's winnings paid for the kids' bedroom furniture. He races the lightest car at the strip — a '53 Falcon Anglia that weighs 2,100 pounds and runs like a scalded cat. He chose the Anglia specifically because nobody else had one. "Why would I want to race the same car as everybody else?" He's an outsider thinker in a conformist world, which makes his Anglia the perfect statement.

## Ambition
Proving that brains and originality beat conformity. Frank chose the Anglia because nobody else had one — he wants to win his own way.
