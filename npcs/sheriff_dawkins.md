## Backstory
Sheriff Dawkins is the top law enforcement officer in Potter County. Deputy Morris works under him. The sheriff's station is on First Street, and the Potter County Impound Lot is behind it — where cars go after street racing busts or unpaid tickets. The sheriff represents the institutional authority that the racing community has to navigate. The sanctioned drag strip exists partly to keep racing off the streets and out of the sheriff's jurisdiction.
