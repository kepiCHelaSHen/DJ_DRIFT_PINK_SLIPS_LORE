## Backstory
Peggy is one of the first girls to stage at the strip. She's tough and determined — she ran an eighteen flat in a stock '53 Falcon Bel-Star and didn't apologize to anyone for the time. She's secretly dating Bobby Meacham (#42), and they're both keeping it quiet from their parents. Her father is a cop — Deputy Morris's partner — which makes the secrecy critical. If he finds out about Bobby and the racing, multiple people are in trouble. Kenmore High class of '57.

### The Frost Top
Regular at the Frost Top on Thursday nights — part of the unofficial girls' night circle with Bonnie, Diane, Rita, Etta, Lindsay, and eventually Ruby. Peg secretly wants to leave town. Her fear, spoken at Thursday girls' night: "Getting stuck here forever."

She started the milkshake pact by slamming her milkshake glass down dramatically: "I'm tired of boys deciding who we are." Mrs. Yamamoto brought six fresh milkshakes. "Good rule."

Present the night Ruby finally came inside the Frost Top, 1959. Nobody made a big deal about it. That mattered most.
