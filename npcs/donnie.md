## Backstory
Father Cletus Finch is a drunk who works at the feed store when he's sober. Mother Mary works the night shift at the hospital. Danny's older sister Margaret married young and moved to Greenfield. Danny lives at home but spends most of his time at Eddie's or at the strip. His home life is rough, which is why he clings to Eddie's crew. He dropped out of Kenmore High senior year but got his GED. Danny is the sidekick. He knows it. He's made peace with it. His own car — a primer-gray '52 Falcon — is built from Watkins Salvage parts and held together with hope. It runs fifteens on a good day. He doesn't care about his own racing career — he cares about being part of something. Eddie's crew is his family, and the strip is his home.
