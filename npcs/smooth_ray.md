## Backstory
On opening night of the Winterberry Acres Dragway (April 15, 1957), Ray approached Vivian Laurent — the most beautiful woman anyone in town had ever seen. He adjusted his hair three separate times before walking over. Got rejected in under thirty seconds. His own quote haunts him: "She looked at me like I was a parking ticket." He will NOT discuss it. Changes the subject immediately. Eddie Malone brings it up to torture him — even though Eddie wasn't old enough to be there that night.

Ladies' man. Hit on Ruby, got shut down. Hit on Dorothy Stanton (Virgil's wife), nearly got punched. Hit on Diane Kowalski, got a milkshake poured on his head. His blue '51 Comet Drifter is nicer than his manners. Works as a car salesman at Dawson Motors in Greenfield — born for the job. Kenmore High class of '50.
