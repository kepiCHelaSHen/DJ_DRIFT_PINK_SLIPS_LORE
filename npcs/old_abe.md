## Backstory
Old Abe is Gene Hartwell's (#13) lifelong friend and rival, and Iron Jack's (#3) generational peer. These three men are the foundation of the racing community. Abe raced Gene once on a Tuesday afternoon — lost by six inches after thirty years of wondering who was faster. He retired happy. His '32 Comet Deuce Roadster with a Comet flathead motor is a museum piece. Lorraine Prescott is his wife. Curtis (#24), Mitchell (#28), and Wayne (#85) are his grandchildren. He lives on Elm Avenue.

## Ambition
He's done. Abe achieved peace when he lost to Gene by six inches and knew the answer to a thirty-year question.
