## Backstory
Wife Rosa, daughter Maria (6). Lives on Pine Street. Works at the plant — day shift, union job. Rosa's parents live next door and help with Maria. Quiet, methodical, driven. Pete doesn't talk about racing — he does it. His '54 Falcon Bel-Star is a sleeper that looks stock from twenty feet away. Under the hood is a motor that Theo Rice helped him tune. Pete and Theo are an unlikely pair — the blue-collar plant worker and the college-educated engineer — but they share a love of precision. Their partnership has made Pete the third-fastest car at the strip, behind Henderson and Ruby. Lost to Henderson Cole in 1955 and has been obsessed with a rematch ever since. Runs scenarios in his head. Has tuned his car specifically to beat Henderson's Drifter. The rematch hasn't happened yet because Henderson keeps dodging it. Pete is patient. Pete will wait.

## Ambition
Beat Henderson Cole. The 1955 loss consumes him. He's tuned his car specifically to beat Henderson's Drifter and runs the rematch scenario in his head constantly.

## Burden
Obsession with the Henderson rematch. It drives him but also consumes him — the line between focus and fixation is thin.
