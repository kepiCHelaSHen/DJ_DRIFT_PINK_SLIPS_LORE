## Backstory
Clyde runs Watkins Salvage on Route 4, east of the grain elevator. Four hundred cars, all organized by make. He's gruff and fair, and he knows the value of everything. He sold Eddie Malone (#1) his first car — a wrecked Bel-Star — at a discount because he felt bad for the kid. He's teaching his son Danny (#43) to build cars properly. They're building a '50 Falcon together. His wife is Mabel. The salvage yard is open Monday through Saturday. Clyde is a Tier 4 character by number but functions more as a town institution — everyone passes through his yard eventually.

After Johnny Vale's funeral, Clyde sold the Rocket 88 wreck to Jack Drummond. He's one of maybe three people who know the truth about Jack's car — that Iron Jack's Comet Rocket 88 is Johnny Vale's "LAST CALL" rebuilt from the wreck. Clyde keeps his mouth shut about a lot of things. This is the one he keeps shut about the tightest.

## Ambition
Passing on the trade. Clyde wants Danny to inherit the yard and the knowledge that comes with it.
