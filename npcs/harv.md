## Backstory
Harv is Theo Rice's (#4) older brother and Jimmy Rice's (#74) older cousin. He co-owns Valley Performance, the speed shop on Third Street with a lathe, mill, hone, and dyno — where serious racers get their motors built. Harv is the people person of the Rice family: Theo is the brain, Harv is the mouth. He's better at selling speed than building it, but he knows enough to run the business. Married to Joan with one kid. His '54 Comet Skylark is the only Comet Skylark at the strip and he's proud of it. Tommy Pierce (#7) has been shadowing Harv at Valley Performance on weekends, learning the speed shop trade.

## Ambition
Building Valley Performance into the premier speed shop in Potter County. Harv is a businessman at heart.
