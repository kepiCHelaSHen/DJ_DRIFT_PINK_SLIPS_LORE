## Backstory
Theo Rice has an engineering degree from Cal Poly. He worked at the Comet Motors division in Detroit for two years — real factory engineering, not shade-tree tinkering — before moving to Winterberry Acres in 1954 to be closer to his aging father Walter, a retired schoolteacher. His mother passed in 1952.

He did not attend Kenmore High — he arrived in town as an adult with credentials and a scientific approach to everything. He took a boring job at the county water department because it was available and close to family. Brilliant mind, mundane employment. The Sterling Starliner is where his real intellect goes.

Every pass down the strip is data. Air-fuel ratios, exhaust gas temperatures, launch RPM, shift points — all logged in a notebook, analyzed, and optimized. The other racers think he's taking the fun out of it. His times say otherwise. He's closing the gap on Henderson Cole through pure analysis, which unnerves Henderson because instinct can't be studied but it can be outperformed by method.

His older brother Harvey co-owns Valley Performance. Theo is the brain; Harv is the mouth. Together they complement each other perfectly. His younger cousin Jimmy Rice just started racing, and Theo is teaching him the analytical approach — passing down the methodology like a professor with a graduate student.

## Ambition
To prove that precision beats instinct. To apply engineering discipline to a world that runs on gut feeling and see it win.
