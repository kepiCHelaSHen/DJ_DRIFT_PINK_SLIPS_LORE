## Backstory
Doc Henderson is the town doctor. He delivers babies on weekdays and fixes engines on weekends. His '53 Falcon is a "doctor car" — respectable and stock-looking on the outside, tuned underneath. He diagnoses carburetors and patients with the same method: check the simple things first. Married to Eleanor with three grown kids. Lives on Elm Avenue. He's the only person in town that Henderson Cole (#9) calls "sir." Doc has a working relationship with Theo Rice (#4) — they swap diagnostic techniques for engines and patients.
