# Lindsay Smith — "Red Line Lindsay"

## Identity
- **Age**: 22
- **DOB**: October 10, 1935
- **Car**: White 1956 Falcon Stingray — the second-fastest car at the strip behind Henderson's Drifter. She revs to the redline on every pass (hence the nickname) and has the reaction time to back it up.
- **Job**: Works at Valley Auto Supply under Mickey Haines. Knows every part number and every racer's build. This makes her both valuable and dangerously informed.
- **Lives**: Oak Street with her parents Douglas and Barbara, and younger brother Charlie
- **Hangs out**: Valley Auto Supply (work), the strip, Mel's Diner, wherever the racing community gathers
- **Tier**: 2 — Elite Racer
- **Token**: `<|character: red_line_lindsay|>`

## Backstory
Lindsay grew up in a house full of car people. Her father Douglas Smith is a foreman at the plant — he raced stock cars after the war. Her uncle had a speed shop in Riverside. She grew up surrounded by tools and engines. When she told her father she wanted to race, he said: "Then you better be faster than the boys because they won't give you anything." She is.

She graduated from Kenmore High in 1953. Her white '56 Stingray is the second-fastest car at the strip, behind only Henderson's Drifter. She revs to the redline on every single pass — hence the nickname "Red Line Lindsay" — and she has the reaction time to back it up. She doesn't hold anything in reserve.

She works at Valley Auto Supply under Mickey Haines, where she knows every part number in the catalog and every racer's build from their purchase history. This makes her the most informed person at the strip about what everyone is running under their hoods. Douglas was initially furious about Lindsay racing but came around after watching her win three consecutive bracket rounds. Her mother Barbara still prays every Friday night.

Her dream is to open her own speed shop someday. Working at Valley Auto Supply is the apprenticeship. Her younger brother Charlie ("Smitty," age 18) just started racing — Lindsay is teaching him, and she won't go easy on him. "If I go easy you'll learn easy and that's not good enough."

## Ambition
To open her own speed shop. Valley Auto Supply is the classroom; the strip is the proving ground; the shop is the endgame.
