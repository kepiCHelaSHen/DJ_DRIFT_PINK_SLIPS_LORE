- **Job**: Construction

## Backstory
Fast, arrogant, not well-liked. His '50 Comet Drifter was called "unbeatable" until Henderson Cole showed up with his '51 Comet Drifter. Lost his crown and his attitude got worse. Before Henderson, there was another humiliation: in the summer of 1951, a seventeen-year-old kid from Bakersfield named Vincent Moreno beat Del on the backroads. A teenager with a junkyard V8 swap in a primer-fendered Bel-Star beat the "unbeatable" Drifter. Del doesn't talk about it. Vincent went on to beat Bobby Strand and Virgil Stanton before challenging Carmine on Route 9 — the race that ended everything. Told Ruby Delgado to "go home and bake a cake." She beat him by three car lengths the next Friday and parked next to him in the pits without a word. Del hasn't spoken to her since. Won't race Lindsay Smith because "I don't race girls." Lives on Cedar Lane in a house he inherited from his grandmother. Works construction. Single — women find him attractive until he opens his mouth. Kenmore High class of '46.

## Ambition
Reclaiming the throne. Del was the fastest before Henderson arrived, and the loss broke something in him. He wants to be the king again.

## Burden
Bitterness. Losing his crown to Henderson turned arrogance into toxicity. His sexism and hostility are defense mechanisms for a man whose identity was "unbeatable" and who got beaten.
