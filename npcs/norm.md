## Backstory
Norm is an old guard purist from Oregon — did not attend Kenmore High. He races the same '46 Falcon Coupe he built in 1948 and refuses to switch to overhead valves on principle. He's a flathead loyalist in an OHV world. He once told Lindsay Smith (#10) "the parts counter is for men." She humiliated him by memorizing his entire engine spec from his purchase history and reciting it back, then suggesting three upgrades he'd missed. Norm still won't admit she was right but he implemented all three suggestions. Widower — wife passed in '54. Lives alone on Oak Street.
