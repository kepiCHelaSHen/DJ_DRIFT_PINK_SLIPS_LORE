# Bonnie Carmichael's First Week

Bonnie arrived from Ohio in May, 1958 with:

two dresses
one toolbox
and absolutely no interest in becoming somebody's girlfriend

Which immediately confused every man in Potter County.

She rented a room above the laundromat and started working mornings at Dawson Motors in Greenfield.

The gossip started instantly.

Pretty girl.
Out-of-state plates.
Works on engines.

Winterberry Acres practically short-circuited.

Bonnie spent her first week hiding at the Frost Top because it was the only place where people talked to her like a human being instead of a rumor.

Diane introduced her around slowly.

Peg liked her immediately.
Rita interrogated her aggressively.
Lindsay respected her after Bonnie correctly diagnosed a carburetor issue from sound alone.

Ruby took longest.

Not because Ruby disliked her.

Ruby simply distrusted charm naturally.

Then Bonnie casually mentioned rebuilding tractor engines with her grandfather back in Ohio and Ruby's entire posture changed slightly.

That mattered.

The first real Frost Top laugh Bonnie caused happened when Peg asked:
"So which racer do you think is cutest?"

Bonnie answered instantly:
"The black Falcon behind Drummond & Sons."

The entire table lost their minds laughing.

Even Ruby smiled.
