# What the Girls Talk About

Men at Winterberry Acres believed women talked about racers constantly.

They were only half right.

The women at the Frost Top talked about:

money
escape
jobs
parents
fear
babies
loneliness
lipstick
factory work
dreams
sex
reputation
marriage
exhaustion

And yes…
occasionally racers.

But usually not the way men imagined.

Thursday nights became unofficial "girls' night" at the Frost Top sometime during 1958.

No formal rule.
It just happened.

Bonnie.
Diane.
Peg.
Rita.
Etta.
Sometimes Lindsay.
Eventually even Ruby once in a while.

Booth near the jukebox.
Extra fries.
Cherry Cokes.

The conversations drifted everywhere.

Rita knew everybody's business.
Diane observed everything.
Bonnie still viewed Winterberry Acres with outsider curiosity.
Peg secretly wanted to leave town.
Etta pretended not to care about anything while caring deeply.

Lindsay mostly listened.

Ruby listened even harder.

One summer night the topic turned dangerous:
"What scares you most?"

Peg answered first.

"Getting stuck here forever."

Bonnie:
"Getting married before I know who I am."

Diane:
"Watching everybody become their parents."

Rita shocked everyone with:
"Being ordinary."

Even Lindsay looked up at that one.

Then somebody asked Ruby.

Long silence.

Finally:
"Needing racing more than happiness."

Nobody joked after that.

Because every girl there understood exactly what she meant.
