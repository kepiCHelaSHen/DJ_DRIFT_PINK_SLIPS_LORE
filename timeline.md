# Timeline of Winterberry Acres

The definitive chronology. If a date appears here, it's canon. If an NPC file contradicts this, this file wins. All other files reference this timeline.

## Pre-War

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| ~1830s | Miller's Crossing (later Crybaby Bridge) built over the ravine on the backroads outside town |
| 1890s | Winterberry Acres founded as agricultural community |
| 1908 | Falcon Motor Company founded in Detroit |
| 1910 | Jack Drummond born, Tennessee hills (January 8) |

## The War Years (1941-1945)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1942 | Jack Drummond enlists, serves in the Pacific with Gene Hartwell |
| 1943-45 | Jack and Gene serve together. What they saw bonds them for life. |
| 1945 | Jack and Gene come west with $400 mustering-out pay. Start working garages. |

## Post-War Racing Scene (1946-1952)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1947 | Gene Hartwell builds his '34 Comet Deuce. Still racing it in 1959. |
| 1948 | Jack Drummond racing the Deuce on backroads. Photo in the garage: Jack beside the chopped Deuce, covered in dirt and victory decals. |
| Summer 1949 | **CRYBABY BRIDGE.** Two racers, one bridge, midnight. A car goes through the guardrail into the ravine. No body found. The bridge gets its name. Jack and Gene climb into the ravine with flashlights. Carmine was on the backroads that night and heard the crash. (Story #1) |
| 1949 | Jack Drummond opens Drummond & Sons Garage on Third Street. Named "& Sons" because he liked the sound. |
| 1950 | **TONY MARCONE ARRIVES.** Black Atlas Box truck, midnight rainstorm, pool hall on Vine Street. Nicky Ferris counting money when the bell jingled. Charlie Blevins hits the floor. "Now you know enough." Within a week, Tony maps every debt in Potter County. (Story #5) |
| 1950 | Jack's wife Helen leaves — can't compete with the cars. Jack never remarries. |
| Summer 1951 | **ROUTE 9 ACCIDENT.** Carmine Ferraro (31) races Vincent Moreno (17, Bakersfield) on Route 9 near Miller Farm. Vincent's pale yellow Falcon Bel-Star hits gravel on the downhill bend, launches into a sycamore tree. Vincent survives with broken spine, crushed pelvis, blind in one eye. "Did I win?" Carmine quits racing that night. (Story #4) |

## Henderson's Rise (1953-1955)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| Late 1953 | **HENDERSON ARRIVES / JOHNNY VALE DIES.** Henderson Cole tows a black Comet Drifter into Winterberry Acres from San Bernardino. Carmine almost turns him away. Two Fridays later: Henderson beats Johnny Vale four straight. Johnny leaves angry, takes Route 9 too fast in the rain. Rocket 88 hits a sycamore tree. Johnny dies. Henderson becomes king. Jack buys the wrecked Rocket 88 from Clyde, rebuilds it. "LAST CALL" under three coats of paint. (Story #2) |
| 1954 | Henderson and Iron Jack's falling out — Henderson refuses a handshake after a loss. Civil now but not warm. |
| 1955 | DJ Moretti's father dies of a heart attack. DJ inherits the drive-in at 23. |

## Ruby's Era (1956)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| March 1956 | Ruby Delgado's father Miguel dies (stomach cancer). Ruby inherits the unfinished Falcon Stingray. |
| Summer 1956 | **RUBY'S FIRST NIGHT.** Ruby shows up at the strip alone. Flagman Earl Patterson laughs. Bobby Strand yells "Try not to stall it, sweetheart!" Ruby runs 11.92 in a naturally aspirated Stingray. "Still funny?" Henderson is the only person who doesn't laugh — he recognizes a killer. (Story #7) |
| Late 1956 | Eddie Malone (21) buys a wrecked '57 Falcon Starliner from Clyde Watkins. Rebuilds it over the winter. |
| December 24, 1956 | **CHRISTMAS EVE TRUCE.** Snow falls on Winterberry Acres — rare enough to be legendary. Every racer gathers at Mel's. Gene started the tradition after punching a man on Dec 23 and Mabel threatening to leave. Henderson sits with people voluntarily for the only time ever. Ruby sits across from him — "most terrifying table in America." Tony Marcone walks in near closing, respects the truce, knows every Christmas lyric. (Story #10) |

## The Dragway Era (1957)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| April 15, 1957 | **OPENING NIGHT.** Winterberry Acres Dragway opens. Gene cuts the ribbon. Angie runs out of buns. Then Vivian Laurent arrives in a pale blue Monarch Continental. Her driver — the stranger with the bleeding hands — races Henderson in a dark green Falcon Starliner. Henderson wins by a hood length. The stranger vanishes. Vivian: "Sometimes it's all people get." (Story #3) |
| July 4, 1957 | **FOURTH OF JULY GRUDGE MATCH.** Henderson vs Darnell Price (Riverside Kid). 500+ spectators. Best 2 of 3. Darnell wins race 1 by a car length ("You finally raced somebody honest"). Henderson wins race 2 by half a hood. Race 3 — pink slips — Darnell's Bel-Star twitches near the finish. Henderson wins. Hands the pink slip back: "Keep the car. Because if you'd stayed in it another second, you were dead." Darnell disappears west on Route 9. (Story #6) |
| Fall 1957 | **JACK'S LAST RACE.** Speedball (Dennis Kohl) mouths off in Jack's garage. "Maybe legends just sound faster because everybody else got old too." Jack brings out the old Comet Deuce. Wins by two car lengths. "Because I stopped trying to prove I was fast a long time ago." Never races seriously again. "There's a difference between loving racing and needing it." (Story #11) |
| Late 1957 | **VINCENT MORENO DIES.** Complications from the 1951 crash. Carmine attends the funeral alone. Nobody knows — not even Angie. Drives to the empty runway and sits for two hours. The dragway idea crystallizes fully. Every rule at the strip is written in somebody's blood. |

## The Reckoning (1958)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| August 2, 1958 | **THE RAID.** Deputy Morris leads county and state police to the dragway. The Miller boy (16) wrapped his brother's Bel-Star around a telephone pole. Sheriff Donnelly's ultimatum. Carmine vs Morris: "You shut this place down and they go back to Route 9!" / "And if I DON'T shut it down, somebody dies here instead." 12 cars impounded including Ruby's Stingray. Henderson's Drifter untouched — Morris found a newspaper clipping about Vincent Moreno on the passenger seat. Winterberry Acres stops feeling invincible. (Story #9) |
| October 18, 1958 | **BIGGEST PINK SLIP RACE.** Henderson vs Leon "Lucky" Serrano. Leon's gold Falcon Starliner runs 11-flat. Challenge at Mel's counter — both titles on the counter. Leon glances sideways at 120 mph, crashes into the barrier. Henderson gives the pink slip back: "Because whatever owns you already took enough." Looks at Tony Marcone when he says it. Only time Tony is visibly angry in public. (Story #8) |

## 1959 — The Player Arrives

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1959 | **THE GAME BEGINS.** The player arrives in Winterberry Acres. All of the above is history, discovered through conversations with NPCs at different trust levels. Henderson is still king. Tony Marcone's operation is growing. Darnell Price hasn't been seen since the Fourth of July. The strip is under pressure from the county. And somewhere in Clyde's junkyard, there are cars with stories nobody tells anymore. |

## Recurring Patterns

**Route 9 West** — where mysteries go to die:
- Johnny Vale (1953) — crashed and died
- Vivian Laurent (1957) — disappeared west
- Darnell Price (1957) — disappeared west

**The Twitch** — Carmine's hell:
- Vincent Moreno (1951) — car twitched, crashed, died slowly over 6 years
- The Stranger (opening night 1957) — car twitched, lost, bleeding hands, vanished
- Darnell Price (Fourth of July 1957) — car twitched, lost, Henderson showed mercy

**Henderson's Mercy** — the crown he never wanted:
- Johnny Vale (1953) — took the crown from a dead man
- Darnell (July 1957) — "Keep the car"
- Leon (October 1958) — "Whatever owns you already took enough"
