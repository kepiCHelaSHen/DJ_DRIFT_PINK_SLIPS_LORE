# Collectibles & Discoverable Objects

Physical things that exist in Winterberry Acres. Each one connects to a story. Some are in plain sight. Some are hidden. All of them mean something to somebody.

The player doesn't collect these like coins. They discover them through exploration, conversation, and trust — and each one opens a door into the history of the town.

## How Discovery Works

Objects aren't marked on a map. They're found through:

1. **Exploration** — walking through Clyde's junkyard, looking around Drummond's garage, examining Henderson's car
2. **Conversation** — an NPC mentions something at high trust, and now the player knows to look for it
3. **Observation** — noticing paint bubbling on Jack's Rocket 88 on a hot day, seeing a folded paper on Henderson's passenger seat
4. **Cross-referencing** — Mel mentions the stranger's green Starliner, Clyde mentions a car in row 7 nobody touches, the player connects the dots

The best discoveries come from combining information from multiple NPCs. No single person has the whole story. The player assembles truth from fragments.
