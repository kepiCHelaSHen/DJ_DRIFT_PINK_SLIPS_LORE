# Winterberry Acres Vehicle Bible

The cars of DJ DRIFT'S PINK SLIPS. All manufacturers and model names are fictional, inspired by the golden age of American automotive design (1932–1959). This document is the single source of truth for every vehicle in the game. The 3D pipeline, NPC lore files, and training data all reference these names.

---

## Manufacturers

### Falcon Motor Company
**Headquarters**: Detroit, Michigan
**Founded**: 1908
**Slogan**: "Built for the Road Ahead"

The everyman brand. Falcon makes the cars that half of America drives — sedans, wagons, pickups, and the occasional surprise. Reliable, affordable, and endlessly customizable. The backbone of Winterberry Acres. If you see a car on Main Street, odds are it's a Falcon.

*Inspired by: Ford + Chevrolet (the volume brands)*

### Comet Motors
**Headquarters**: Flint, Michigan
**Founded**: 1920
**Slogan**: "Nothing Faster Under the Stars"

The performance brand. Comet makes cars for people who want to go fast and look good doing it. Their engines are legendary at the strip — the Comet V8 is the heart of half the hot rods in California. Flashier than Falcon, cheaper than Monarch. The racer's choice.

*Inspired by: Pontiac + Mercury + performance Chevy*

### Monarch Motorworks
**Headquarters**: Detroit, Michigan
**Founded**: 1902
**Slogan**: "The Standard of Excellence"

The luxury brand. Monarch makes the cars that doctors, lawyers, and bank managers drive. Long, elegant, chrome-heavy. The '59 Monarch Imperial is the most ostentatious car ever built — tail fins you could hang laundry on. Not common at the strip, but when one shows up, people notice.

*Inspired by: Cadillac + Lincoln + Packard + DeSoto*

### Sterling Automotive
**Headquarters**: South Bend, Indiana
**Founded**: 1911
**Slogan**: "Engineered to Impress"

The middle-class aspirational brand. Sterling makes cars for people who want something nicer than a Falcon but can't afford a Monarch. Known for distinctive design — portholes, sweepspear body lines, jet-age styling. The brand for people who care about how their car looks.

*Inspired by: Buick + Oldsmobile + Studebaker*

### Atlas Motors
**Headquarters**: Akron, Ohio
**Founded**: 1915
**Slogan**: "Built to Work"

The commercial and utility brand. Atlas makes the trucks, vans, buses, and work vehicles that keep America running. Every delivery truck, fire engine, and tow truck in Winterberry Acres is an Atlas. Not glamorous, but indestructible.

*Inspired by: International Harvester + GMC + White Motor Company*

### Imported Brands

**Hertzig** (Germany) — The little economy car. Round, rear-engined, oddly lovable. The kids call them "bugs."
*Inspired by: Volkswagen Beetle*

**Lumière** (France) — French jet-age engineering. The DS is the most futuristic car on the road. Nobody in Winterberry Acres knows how to fix one.
*Inspired by: Citroën*

**Kabinenroller** (Germany) — A three-wheeled bubble car with a canopy cockpit. More motorcycle than car. A curiosity.
*Inspired by: Messerschmitt KR200*

## Pipeline Reference

The `Pipeline ID` column maps directly to the chassis filenames in the 3D pipeline:
- `sedan_55` → `sedan_55.glb`, `sedan_55_cherry_red.glb`, etc.
- Model names are used in NPC dialogue, lore files, and training data
- When an NPC says "my Bel-Star", the player sees `sedan_55` in the garage

## Quick Reference for Training Data

When generating NPC dialogue about cars, use these terms:
- "Bel-Star" not "Bel Air"
- "Starliner" not "Bel Air coupe" or "hardtop"
- "Stingray" not "Corvette"
- "Drifter" not "Mercury" or "Hudson"
- "Deuce" not "Ford coupe"
- "Turbo-Fire" not "small block Chevy"
- "Rocket 88" not "Olds" or "Oldsmobile"
- "Falcon" not "Chevy" or "Ford"
- "Comet" not "Mercury" or "Pontiac"
- "Monarch" not "Cadillac" or "Lincoln"

NPCs talk about cars constantly. These names must be second nature in every training example.
